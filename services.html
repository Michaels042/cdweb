Clarifications & Assurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding property verification, title checks, construction supervision, and management agreements.

How do you verify land ownership and titles before marketing?

+
As detailed in our 5-step sales workflow, we require formal written owner authorization and collect original survey plans, deeds, and title records. We then facilitate independent legal searches at the relevant State Ministry of Lands (e.g. Enugu State Ministry of Lands and Urban Development) and engage registered surveyors to cross-check beacons on-site to ensure zero government acquisition or dispute.

Can diaspora clients purchase land or build without being physically present?

+
Yes, absolutely. A significant portion of our clientele resides outside Nigeria. We provide video inspections, geo-tagged survey documentation, transparent escrow coordination through appointed legal practitioners, and regular weekly site progress video updates during building and construction phases.

What is included in your property management service?

+
Our asset management mandate covers tenant screening and onboarding, prompt rent collection and financial remittance, routine maintenance supervision, utility monitoring, legal lease documentation, and regular structural condition assessments to preserve your property's long-term market value.

How do you coordinate with architects, engineers, and builders?

+
We work strictly with certified professionals registered with COREN, ARCON, and QSRBN. Our team manages project assessment, Bills of Quantities (BOQ), statutory building permits with town planning authorities, and milestone-by-milestone quality assurance inspections through to final handover.
