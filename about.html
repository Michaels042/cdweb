Company Background

Built On Trust, Transparency, and Professionalism

We connect clients to land, homes, rental opportunities, and property-related services through structured processes, clear communication, and client-first support.

CD’s Landmark Properties was established with a singular conviction: real estate transactions should be transparent, verified, and empowering for clients. Whether a client is purchasing their first residential plot, expanding commercial facilities, or investing in multi-family rental buildings, they deserve objective facts, rigorous documentation, and dependable support.

Our reach is Nigeria-wide, with a strong operational and market focus on Eastern Nigeria (including Enugu, Anambra, Imo, Abia, and Ebonyi States). We support property enquiries and developments across the region while actively executing suitable assignments and partnerships across other parts of the federation.

Our multidisciplinary team bridges the gap between private property owners, certified surveyors, structural engineers, architects, and legal practitioners — ensuring every property journey is seamless and legally sound.
